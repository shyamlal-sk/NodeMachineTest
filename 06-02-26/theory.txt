### Q5. Answer the following

a) What is **asynchronous programming**?  

    In asynchronous programming, one task doesnt wait for another task to finish.
    Multiple operations are run at a time. It is non sequential.

b) What is the **call stack**?  

    Call stack executes tasks continuosly, receiving tasks to do
    from event loop, in given priority.
   
c) What is the **event loop**?  

    Event loop orchastrates the management of tasks in callstack
    and queues to move them between callstack, micro/macro task and callback queues. 
    it watches callstack if it becomes free and gives it more tasks.
    
d) What is a **microtask**?  

    micro tasks such as promises are called first from a callstack.

e) What is a **macrotask**?

    macro tasks such as setTimout(), runs only after microtasks are run.
